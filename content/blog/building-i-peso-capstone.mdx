For my main capstone project as a 4th-year IT student at Urdaneta City University, I'm taking on something ambitious: **i-PESO, A Smart Employment System for Urdaneta City**. 

The goal is to digitize and drastically improve the Public Employment Service Office (PESO) workflow. We wanted to build a digital bridge that connects local job seekers with employers, while giving the local government the tools they need to track labor market analytics and automate their DOLE (Department of Labor and Employment) reporting.

As the lead programmer and developer, I'm building this across three distinct platforms.

## A true multi-platform architecture

A system like this can't just be a single web app. It has to meet users where they are:
1. **The Web Frontend (React + Vite):** Designed for employers to manage their company profiles, post jobs, and process applicants using a Kanban-style Applicant Tracking System (ATS).
2. **The Mobile App (React Native + Expo):** Built for the job seekers. This is where they build their profiles, browse opportunities, and apply for jobs on the go.
3. **The Backend (Laravel + MySQL):** The central nervous system. It handles the API requests, business logic, authentication, and the complex relational data required for employment tracking.

## AI-powered matching and Kanban ATS

One of the biggest pain points in recruitment is matching the right skills to the right job requirements. Instead of relying on simple keyword searches, I'm integrating **AI and skill-based job matching**. The system actively analyzes a job seeker's profile and pushes the most relevant opportunities to their mobile feed.

On the employer side, I wanted to move away from messy spreadsheet tracking. I built a **Kanban-style ATS** directly into the web dashboard. Employers can drag and drop applicants through different stages (Applied, Interviewing, Hired, Rejected), making the recruitment pipeline entirely visual.

## Digitizing the Job Fair

Job fairs are notoriously chaotic. Paper resumes get lost, and tracking who hired who is a nightmare for the PESO office.

To solve this, I'm developing a **Hybrid Job Fair + QR system**. Job seekers use their i-PESO mobile app to generate a unique QR code. When they visit an employer's booth at a physical job fair, the employer simply scans the code to instantly receive the applicant's digital profile and resume. It seamlessly blends physical networking with digital tracking.

## Automating the red tape

A massive part of PESO's responsibility is generating labor market analytics and submitting mandatory reports to DOLE. Historically, this involves hours of manual data collation. Because all employment activity now flows through the i-PESO Laravel backend, the system can automatically generate and export formatted DOLE reports and live labor market analytics with a single click.

## Where it stands today

Between February and March 2026, we locked down the project scope, core objectives, and technical architecture, and established our public GitHub repository. 

Right now, i-PESO is in active, heavy development. It's easily the most complex system I've built to date—juggling state across a React frontend, a mobile app, and a robust Laravel backend—but seeing the pieces connect and realizing the real-world impact this could have on Urdaneta City makes the grind entirely worth it.
