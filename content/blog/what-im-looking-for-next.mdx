Most portfolio sites make you guess what the person actually wants. This post is the opposite of that. If you found me through LinkedIn or a job application, this is the short version of what I'm after and what you'd be getting.

## Where I am

I'm in my fourth year of a BS in Information Technology at Urdaneta City University, graduating in 2027. I'm based in Pangasinan, Philippines, and I work remotely without any friction — the freelance work I already do is remote.

That means I'm looking for things that fit alongside a final year of coursework: internships, part-time or contract development work, freelance design commissions, and ongoing virtual assistant engagements. After 2027, full-time.

## The three things I do

I'm a generalist, and I've stopped apologising for it. It's the most useful thing about me on a small team, where the person who can build the thing, design the thing, and then answer the emails about the thing is worth more than three specialists you can't afford yet.

**Development.** React and Next.js on the front end, Laravel and Node.js on the back, Flutter and Dart for mobile with Firebase behind it. I've shipped a full-stack barangay information system with eight role-scoped dashboards, a dual-screen Python photobooth application that runs live at events, and a Flutter e-commerce app with both a storefront and an admin side.

**Design.** UI/UX, branding and graphic design in Figma, Photoshop, Illustrator and Canva, plus 3D work in Blender. I've been doing paid design work since Grade 12, and I've competed in it — champion in tarpaulin making at Cluster VI Technolympics and second at the division level, both in 2023.

**Support.** Customer support, social media management, email and calendar management, and data entry. This is the lane people are most surprised to see next to the other two, and it's the one I'd argue for hardest. It's the work that decides whether a small business feels organised or chaotic.

## What you'd actually be hiring

A few things that are true across all three lanes:

- **I finish.** Two IT Olympics runs in the Computer Network Category, an elected SK council seat since 2023, and a PRO role in the Junior Information Technology Society all involve showing up repeatedly over months. That's the same muscle as shipping.
- **I can write.** Clear replies to customers, captions that don't sound like a template, commit messages, documentation. My film background — including a Best Director win at the Orata Film Festival — shows up here more than anywhere: pacing and structure apply as much to a support reply as to a scene.
- **I ask before I assume.** On the barangay system, the eight dashboards exist because a Captain, a Treasurer, and a Tanod need genuinely different things. That only becomes obvious if you ask.

## What I'm not

I'm not going to claim deep specialisation I don't have. I'm early-career, I've built real things but not at scale, and I'd rather say that than oversell and disappoint you in week three. If you need someone with five years of production Kubernetes, that isn't me.

What I am is someone who picks things up quickly and doesn't need to be chased.
